canarybird {{ .Values.version }} is rolling out as {{ include "canarybird.fullname" . }} in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Open the grid:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "canarybird.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} charts/canarybird --reuse-values --set version=v2 --set color=green

Then break only the new version from the panel ("Apply to: v2 only" → error-spike)
and watch your rollout controller react.
